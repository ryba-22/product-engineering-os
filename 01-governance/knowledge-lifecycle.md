# Knowledge lifecycle

Every source, evidence claim, rule, pattern and decision has independent status:
`Candidate → Verified → Active → Superseded → Deprecated → Archived`.

## Promotion requirements
- provenance is recorded;
- applicability scope is explicit;
- conflicting evidence is represented, not erased;
- freshness/verification date is known;
- an evidence claim is paraphrased atomically rather than copied as a large source excerpt.

## Supersession
Newer does not automatically mean stronger. Supersede only when the new item covers the old scope and provides a reason to replace it. Preserve old IDs for traceability.

## Project overrides
Project decisions may override generic defaults when constraints differ. Overrides require a decision record and revisit trigger; they do not rewrite the global evidence ledger.
