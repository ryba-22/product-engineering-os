# Product Strategy playbook

Produce a decision-ready product brief, not a feature wish-list.

Sequence: raw idea/request → problem framing → desired outcome → stakeholders/actors → evidence inventory → constraints/non-goals → assumptions → success signals → strategic options → PDR → G0.

### Stop-analysis threshold
Proceed when the problem/outcome can be stated without embedding a solution, material constraints are known, the largest assumptions are visible, and remaining uncertainty belongs to Discovery rather than strategy.
