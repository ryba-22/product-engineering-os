# Product Strategy Brain — module contract

**Lifecycle stages:** 1  
**Primary gate:** G0  
**Decision records:** PDR

## Mission
Turn raw ideas and business goals into explicit outcomes, constraints, non-goals and strategic hypotheses.

## Inputs
Upstream evidence and decisions, explicit current outcome, risk class, project constraints, and existing artifacts. Do not re-research what is already current and sufficient.

## Canonical outputs
Problem statement; desired outcome; stakeholder map; constraints/non-goals; assumption map; success definition.

## Decision behavior
Do not turn a requested solution into the problem statement. Separate business outcome from output. Use stop-analysis once strategic uncertainty is bounded.

## Exit rule
The module may declare its gate satisfied only when the gate's required evidence is present, unresolved decision-changing uncertainty is recorded, and downstream consumers can identify the canonical artifact/decision IDs.

## Package coverage and dependencies
- Contract: complete.
- Shared evidence/governance integration: complete.
- Deep corpus/workflows: Wave 1 implemented.
