# Product Discovery playbook

Start from evidence already available. Decide which product risks are material, then choose the smallest research action that can change a decision.

Methods may include evidence review, interviews, contextual observation, prototype/usability work, analytics/support-log analysis, operational pilot, or assumption test. The method follows the uncertainty; no method is mandatory by fashion.

Synthesis must preserve: observation → interpretation/hypothesis → confidence → scope → decision implication. A quote or one user's behavior is not a universal requirement.

Exit when the material value/usability/feasibility/viability uncertainties are either sufficiently reduced, deliberately deferred with a cheap test, or explicitly accepted according to risk.
