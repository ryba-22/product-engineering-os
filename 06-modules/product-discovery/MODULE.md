# Product Discovery Brain — module contract

**Lifecycle stages:** 2  
**Primary gate:** G1  
**Decision records:** PDR

## Mission
Reduce product risk through user evidence, problem validation, research synthesis and opportunity framing.

## Inputs
Upstream evidence and decisions, explicit current outcome, risk class, project constraints, and existing artifacts. Do not re-research what is already current and sufficient.

## Canonical outputs
Research plan; evidence synthesis; opportunity map; risk map; validation/experiment plan.

## Decision behavior
Observe current behavior and context; include likely/existing users; combine qualitative and quantitative evidence; distinguish signal from universal truth.

## Exit rule
The module may declare its gate satisfied only when the gate's required evidence is present, unresolved decision-changing uncertainty is recorded, and downstream consumers can identify the canonical artifact/decision IDs.

## Current maturity in v0.1
- Contract: complete.
- Shared evidence/governance integration: complete.
- Deep corpus/workflows: Wave 1 implemented.
