# Continuous Feedback / Learning Loop

## Inputs
Product analytics, user research, support/contact reasons, operational workarounds, search/log data, errors/incidents, sales/implementation feedback, accessibility findings and delivery/reliability signals.

## Loop
1. Ingest evidence with provenance and scope.
2. Cluster signals around outcomes/problems, not around requested features.
3. Separate observation from interpretation and hypothesis.
4. Update opportunity/problem model and confidence.
5. Choose the highest-value uncertainty or friction on the current outcome.
6. Run the cheapest valid discovery/experiment/implementation slice.
7. Measure outcome and guardrails.
8. Record learning, supersede decisions if needed, and repeat.

Avoid backlog-as-graveyard. Every promoted opportunity should link to outcome/evidence and have a decision horizon.
