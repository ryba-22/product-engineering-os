# Experimentation Brain

## Choose the evidence method first
Use the cheapest method that can validly resolve the uncertainty: interview/observation, prototype/usability test, fake-door/concierge test, operational pilot, shadow mode, staged rollout, controlled A/B experiment or production measurement.

## When A/B fits
A causal product question, enough eligible traffic/time, stable assignment, measurable outcomes/guardrails, acceptable ethical/operational risk and instrumentation quality.

## Experiment contract
Hypothesis; decision to be made; unit of randomization; eligibility/trigger; control/treatment; primary metric; guardrails; minimum detectable effect/power assumptions where needed; duration/stopping policy; data-quality checks; analysis plan; rollout/rollback plan.

## Trustworthiness gates
Check assignment/exposure and Sample Ratio Mismatch before interpreting outcomes. Do not peek/stop opportunistically without a predeclared valid sequential method. Distinguish statistical evidence from product importance.
